# Landing scroll sections

## Description
Serves US-5 (UI/UX). Below the hero, the landing page explains the game with "How it works" and "Modes" lists of text. This task replaces them with short scroll sections, one big statement each, that animate as the player scrolls, so the page explains the game with very little text. Design: "D · B + C mix" on the landing page canvas.

## Specs
- The sections, in order: "Two teams", "Same problems", a clock, "One winner", then "Your move" with "Find a match" and "Private lobby".
- Each statement scales in and comes into focus as it reaches the middle of the screen, then fades as it leaves.
- "Two teams": a dot per player slides in from each side, with "1v1 · 2v2 · 3v3" under it. No "vs".
- "Same problems": Easy, Medium and Hard labels.
- Clock: a clock face whose hand turns a full 360° and fills a ring as the player scrolls. It starts while "Same problems" is still on screen and only completes when the player has scrolled through the section. "Every second counts" appears once it completes. No "30:00".
- A thin bar at the top of the page shows how far the player has scrolled.
- Sections are short, so the page does not take much scrolling.
- Browsers without scroll-driven animations, and players with reduced motion, see the same sections without the animation.
- No periods on the landing page, except in text of two lines or more.

## Requirements
- The sections live in `frontend/app/_landing/`, replacing the "How it works" and "Modes" sections of `landing-page.tsx`.
- Animations are CSS only (scroll-driven animations with a fallback). No new dependencies.
- Use only the theme classes defined in docs/frontend/theming.md.
- Frontend tests (`frontend/test/`) do not touch backend services.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The landing page shows "Two teams", "Same problems", "One winner" and "Your move", in that order.
2. "How it works" and "Modes" are no longer shown.
3. "Two teams" shows "1v1 · 2v2 · 3v3" and no "vs".
4. "Same problems" shows Easy, Medium and Hard.
5. The clock section shows a clock and "Every second counts", and no "30:00".
6. "Your move" has "Find a match" and "Private lobby" links.
7. The scroll progress bar is hidden from screen readers.
8. The landing page text has no periods.
