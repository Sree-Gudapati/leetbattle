# Landing hero and match demo

## Description
Serves US-5 (UI/UX). The landing page opens with a long slogan and a paragraph of text. This task replaces the hero with the LeetBattle wordmark drawn in by the same pen-tip wipe as the page loader, and replaces the live demo card with a short looping match: two teams code the same problem, Team 1 finishes first, submits, passes and wins. Design: "D · B + C mix" on the landing page canvas.

## Specs
- The hero shows only the LeetBattle wordmark, one short line under it, "Find a match" and "Private lobby". No slogan and no paragraph.
- On load the wordmark is revealed left to right behind a green pen tip, with the same timing as the page loader's pen wipe. The pen tip then stays after the wordmark and blinks like a text cursor.
- Under the hero, a match demo shows Team 1 and Team 2 side by side, each typing the same problem.
- Team 1 types faster. Its Submit button presses, shows "Running", then "Passed", the test results appear and Team 1's score goes from 0 to 100.
- Team 2's side fades and "Team 1 wins" fades in, with no box around it. The loop then starts again.
- The demo tilts flat as it scrolls into view.
- With reduced motion, the wordmark, cursor, code and result are shown without animation.
- No periods on the landing page, except in text of two lines or more.

## Requirements
- The hero lives in `frontend/app/_landing/landing-page.tsx`; the demo replaces `frontend/app/_landing/live-demo.tsx`.
- Reuse the page loader's `lb-wipe` / `lb-tip` timing from `frontend/app/globals.css`. The final wipe must not clip the last letter.
- Animations are CSS only. No new dependencies.
- Use only the theme classes defined in docs/frontend/theming.md.
- Frontend tests (`frontend/test/`) do not touch backend services.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The landing page shows the LeetBattle wordmark as its main heading, and no slogan.
2. The wordmark has a pen-tip cursor after it.
3. "Find a match" and "Private lobby" are shown.
4. The demo shows a Team 1 panel and a Team 2 panel.
5. The demo shows Submit, Running and Passed states for Team 1.
6. The demo shows Team 1's score of 100 and "Team 1 wins".
7. With reduced motion the wordmark and demo are shown in their finished state.
8. The landing page text has no periods.
