# Compact scoreboard

## Description
Serves US-5 (UI/UX). The scoreboard above the editor takes up a lot of height: a "Time left" label over the clock, and a list of the five most recent submissions beside it. This task shrinks it to one line with just the two team scores and the time left, so the editor gets the space.

## Specs
- The scoreboard is one line: Team 1 and its score, the time left, then Team 2 and its score.
- The recent submissions list (and "No submissions yet") is no longer shown.
- The "Time left" label is no longer shown; screen readers still hear the timer as "Time left".
- The scores keep their team colours and the timer still counts down every second.

## Requirements
- The change is in `frontend/app/editor/components/scoreboard.tsx`.
- Use only the theme classes defined in docs/frontend/theming.md. No new dependencies.
- Frontend tests (`frontend/test/scoreboard.test.tsx`) do not touch backend services.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The scoreboard shows Team 1's score, the time left and Team 2's score.
2. The recent submissions list and "No submissions yet" are not shown.
3. No "Time left" label is shown, but the timer is labelled "Time left" for screen readers.
4. The scores keep their colours and the timer counts down.
