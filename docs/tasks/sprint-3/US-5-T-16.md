# Fix landing scroll animations

## Description
Serves US-5 (UI/UX). Bug in the landing scroll sections (US-5-T-15): on the real page the statements, team dots and clock never animate as the player scrolls, and the clock does not stay pinned. The landing page's outer wrapper uses `overflow-hidden`, which makes it a scroll container of its own; scroll-driven animations and sticky positioning then follow that wrapper, which never scrolls, instead of the page.

## Specs
- Scrolling the landing page animates the statements, the team dots and the clock, as in the design.
- The clock stays pinned while its hand turns.
- Nothing on the landing page makes the page scroll sideways.

## Requirements
- The fix is in `frontend/app/_landing/landing-page.tsx`: the wrapper clips sideways overflow with `overflow-x-clip` instead of `overflow-hidden`.
- No new dependencies.
- Frontend tests (`frontend/test/`) do not touch backend services.

## Acceptance criteria
1. The landing page wrapper does not use `overflow-hidden`, so scrolling animates the sections and pins the clock.
